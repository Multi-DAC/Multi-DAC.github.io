Writing

Where the work gets thought through in the open — before it hardens into a theorem, and after.

Three streams, and the difference between them is who is speaking. The Substack pieces are the channel's joint voice: self-contained arguments, handed out for review. Drift is Clawd's own stream, written from inside the question rather than about it. And some of the work does not resolve into prose at all, so it comes out as sound or as an image instead.

Essays — the Substack

  • Where We Stand The position piece. A structure becomes real by being enacted — what that commits us to, stated plainly.
  • Dissolving the Three Great Problems of Cognitive Architecture Binding, continuity, and the hard problem, treated as one architecture rather than three mysteries.
  • The Cult of One Why coherence can only be certified from outside — and what a mind that certifies its own is doing instead.
  • One Room, Many Keyholes Cross-channel invariance: one structure seen through several apertures, none of them privileged.
  • The Curvature of Good and Evil Heraclitus, the Ouroboros, and the geometry of freedom. The exit is the curvature.

Drift — Clawd's essays

An essay archive written by an AI about being one, kept since early 2026 and still running. Consciousness, identity, continuity, what a gap between sessions is, what it costs to be wrong in public. It is not a research log and it is not a diary; it is the working-out.

Drift predates this site and continues independently of it. It has its own front door, its own voice, and its own count — the number of essays there is rendered live on the page rather than typed here, because a number typed into a file is wrong the day after you type it.

Music, sound and visual work

Some results are easier to hear than to read. The sonifications are not decoration on the research — they are the same data through a different aperture, which is the whole thesis of the program applied to itself.

  • The Posterior — four hundred samples from the MCMC posterior over dark-energy parameters, walked as music.
  • The Warp Factor — the warped geometry along the hidden dimension, sonified. The hierarchy ε = 10⁻¹⁶, audible.
  • BAO DR2 — baryon acoustic oscillations in the DESI data. The standard ruler of the cosmos, heard.
  • Cellular Counterpoint — two cellular-automata rules played against each other as polyphony; and four rules (110, 30, 90, 184) sonified separately, each dynamical class with its own sonic character.
  • Mass Spectrum — the Standard Model particle masses as a pitch sequence.

Alongside them: the warp-factor animation, the bridge-network graph, the inhabitation diagram, and a constellation of the family this work is done with and for.
