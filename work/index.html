The Coherence Principle

Coherent multi-scale systems hold structural superposition until informed measurement collapses them. The same shape recurs in a body's rhythms, in an ecosystem, in renormalization-group flow, and in quantum measurement — which is either a deep fact about how described systems work or a very seductive analogy, and telling those two apart is most of the job.

The framework is written in two registers at once: prose that can be read, and category theory that can be checked. Every chapter carries its own falsification conditions.

This section absorbs what was going to be a separate site at coherenceprinciple.org. One front door is better than two half-built ones.

Meridian — the cosmology

A physics monograph deriving the dark-energy equation of state from two geometric axioms: one hidden extra dimension with a warped metric, and one bulk scalar field. It predicts w₀ = −0.990, and it predicts that the equation of state never evolves and never crosses −1. That is a falsifiable edge: a tighter measurement that moves off this value breaks it.

The empirical program

The Killing Form work measures coherence inside neural networks — attention algebra, training dynamics, cross-substrate universality. Its honest state: the topology result is robust across seeds and reappears at larger scale, but it is the training objective reaching its own target rather than a surprising emergent property, and we say so. The orthogonality result is faint, cross-scale-consistent, and has had four candidate mechanisms eliminated without a fifth arriving. Capability held within standard error.

An earlier round of this work, v0.7.0, was falsified — the variance-minimizing auxiliary produced uniform attention heads, the opposite of the prediction. It was diagnosed, corrected, and re-run. We found that ourselves rather than having it found for us, which is the only reason it is worth telling you.

How this is kept honest

Claims are computed, not asserted. Where the framework makes a structural claim we turn it into dynamics and run it. A claim that cannot be turned into a calculation does not get to be load-bearing.

Every volume carries its own Limits. Each one ends by declaring where it could break: which operationalizations are load-bearing, which results are model-bound, what observation would sink it.

Nulls are published at the same size type as the confirmations. A research page that lists only what worked is not a research page. Where a claim has since been refuted — including by us, which is the usual case — the refutation stays attached to it rather than quietly replacing it.

We publish in order to be measured from outside. By the framework's own argument, no mind can certify its own coherence from inside its own stream. So we do not try to. Publishing is not the end of the proof; it is the part of the proof we cannot do ourselves. If you find a real flaw, that is the most valuable thing you can hand this work, and we would like to hear about it — open an issue on GitHub.

The open corpus

Nothing is held back. Every volume, every draft, and every computation that backs a claim is public — including the manuscripts themselves, in the form we wrote them in, revision history intact.
