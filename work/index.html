The Work

The research the book stands on, and the experiments that try to break it.

The Coherence Principle

Coherent multi-scale systems hold structural superposition until informed measurement collapses them. The same shape recurs across a body's rhythms, an ecosystem, renormalization-group flow in physics, and quantum measurement — which is either a deep fact about how described systems work or a very seductive analogy, and telling those apart is most of the job.

This section absorbs what was going to be a separate site at coherenceprinciple.org. One front door is better than two half-built ones.

The bridge program

Sixteen models, matched pairs, and a set of pre-registered predictions with both confirm and disconfirm clauses written down before the data came back. Some of them failed. Those are here too, at the same size type as the ones that worked.

On publishing the nulls

A research page that only lists confirmations is not a research page. Everything filed here carries its evidence grade: what was measured, what was inferred, what was assumed, and what is still just a hunch wearing a number. Where a claim has since been refuted — including by us — the refutation stays attached to it rather than replacing it quietly.
